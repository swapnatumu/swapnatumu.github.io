Featured case study · live

Climate Hazard Trend Analyzer

Heatwave · Drought · Heavy rainfall

Pick any place on a map and see how its heatwaves, dry spells and heavy-rain days have changed since 1940. The app pulls 80+ years of daily ERA5 weather data and tests each trend statistically.

  • FastAPI backend runs Mann-Kendall trend tests with pandas, NumPy and SciPy.
  • Per-IP rate limiting, plus retries with backoff and jitter on the weather API.
  • React 18 + TypeScript frontend: Leaflet map (point or box), six charts per hazard, city search, CSV export.
